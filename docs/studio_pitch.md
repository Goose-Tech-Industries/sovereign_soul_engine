# Sovereign Soul Engine
## Living NPCs for your game

Your NPCs are brilliant for the first five minutes and forgettable after ten.
Hand-authored dialogue trees cost a fortune and still feel static the moment the
player walks away. We fix that.

Sovereign Soul Engine gives every NPC a **persistent inner life** — memory,
relationships, moods, grudges, and gossip — that keeps evolving whether the
player is online or not. Characters meet, argue, drift apart, and *remember*.

---

## What you get

**Persistent memory.** An NPC remembers what your player did — the betrayal, the
favor, the promise — for months, across sessions and servers.

**A living world.** NPCs talk to *each other* when the player isn't looking:
alliances, feuds, rumors. Log back in and the blacksmith knows what the barkeep
said about you last night.

**Runs on your hardware.** Local inference, bring-your-own-key, zero per-call
server cost to you. No metered API bill, no "the cloud is down so the world froze."

**Portable souls.** A player's companion exports as a signed `.soul` file. Their
relationship survives servers, sequels, and platforms. Players *own* the bond
they built.

## The safety layer your legal team will ask about

Companion AI is a liability minefield. We've already built the controls:

- **Age gate + informed consent** at the door (18+, "these are generative AI,
  not people or therapists").
- **Safe word** — "code red" freezes a persona instantly, any time.
- **Intimacy ceilings** per relationship archetype.
- **Anti-parasocial circuit breaker** — detects unhealthy dependence and
  de-escalates.
- **Selective amnesia** — surgically purge memories for compliance or user
  request.

The romance and grit, *without* the class-action. That's the product.
