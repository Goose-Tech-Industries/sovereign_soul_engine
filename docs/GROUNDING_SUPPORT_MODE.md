# Grounding support mode

SSE's grounding mode is an explicit opt-in conversation aid. It can offer present-moment orientation, sensory prompts, gentle breathing prompts, reality-based language, and a next safe step.

It does not diagnose a user, infer a psychiatric condition, confirm unusual perceptions as facts, act as a therapist, or replace professional care. Safety signals must be explicit. A child or minor path asks for a trusted adult; immediate danger, self-harm, or harm-to-others signals escalate to a trusted person and local emergency services.

The mode is designed for both real support and fictional companions. A game character may deliver a grounding scene, but the product must clearly distinguish roleplay from real-world support and must never use a player's disclosed condition as hidden character lore.

The sensory and breathing patterns follow established public grounding guidance from the [VA National Center for PTSD](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/treat/essentials/grounding.asp) and [NHS CAMHS](https://mft.nhs.uk/camhs/advice/anxiety/).
